# Researchlyy

Role: Act as a senior software engineer with 5 years of experience in web app building.

Context: We are an online platform that will provide Indian students access to research opportunities and courses in various fields like astronomy research, biological research, economics, robotics, environmental sciences, psychology, history, etc.

Task: Your task is to build a frontend, where you have to build a page in which there are 2 sections divided horizontally, one for research opportunities and one for courses, and a customer can select any of them, which will then be added to the cart. After that, there will be a pop-up when the customer clicks on the cart icon, and there will be a button on which the customer can click and pay. When the customer selects a course/research, there will be a slide popup that will include a small, detailed summary of what that course/research opportunity includes and what the benefits will be in bullet points.

Constraints: It should have 3 screens and should not ask for any personal info other than name and email. Multiple items can be selected.

 

Format: Screen 1 · Home: shows a list of courses available and research opportunities, with a button that has an attractive photo of that field, which, when clicked on, opens the details of it.

Screen 2 · Selected item: shows the user's selected item, with a button shaped like a cart and has the word "Cart" written under it.

Screen 3 · Buy: shows a screen with a list of selected items, with a button shaped like cash that has the word "Pay" written under it that, when clicked, pops an option of payment methods.

 

Example: The app should be easy to use; when the mouse hovers over an item, there should be a small summarized detail of that item and its benefits in bullet points. It should be attractive. It should have a lot of options so that students of various academic backgrounds can use it.
